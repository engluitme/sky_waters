FAQ

Questions We Hear Often

How deep will my borehole be?

Depth depends on local geology and the water table. After a survey we give you an estimate before drilling starts.

Can I run the pump on solar?

Yes. We design and install solar pump systems with panels, tank stand and piping.

Do you service vehicles for fleets?

Yes, our Auto Care division offers scheduled fleet maintenance and repairs.

What general services do you cover?

Plumbing, welding, fabrication, hauling and repairs — contact us with your job.
